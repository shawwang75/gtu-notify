What is GTU Lark Business Notifications?

GTU Lark Business Notifications is a powerful Odoo module that automatically pushes business events to Lark (Feishu), keeping your team informed in real-time. When important business events occur in Odoo, such as task assignments, CRM lead updates, or timesheet modifications, relevant team members receive instant notifications via Lark cards with direct links to the Odoo records.

Key Benefits:

  • ✅ Real-time Notifications - Never miss important business events
  • ✅ Rich Card Messages - Professional-looking notifications with all context
  • ✅ Direct Links - One-click access to Odoo records from Lark
  • ✅ Zero Dependencies - Fully standalone, no other Lark modules required
  • ✅ Easy Configuration - Set up in minutes with simple Lark App configuration
